About The Midas Law

The Midas Law is a plain-English money guide and workbook built for first-generation wealth builders. The guide uses a five-floor ladder: stability, credit and debt, the stock market, compounding and retirement, and street-level economics. The printed guide and workbook identify the copyright holder as IGNIUM LLC (© 2026).

This site, themidaslawbook.com, hosts the Guide and Workbook PDFs, a crawlable copy of the guide’s glossary, and a few full chapters from the guide as HTML. An interactive web app is described on the home page and is labeled coming soon; it is not available yet.

The Guide and Workbook are free PDF downloads. This site does not process payments.
